This policy explains what information is collected when you use www.learnforgex.com ("the site"), what it is used for, and what choices you have.

## Who operates the site

LearnForgeX is an independent learning platform operated by Cemalettin Durgun. For anything concerning your personal data, write to [learnforgex@gmail.com](mailto:learnforgex@gmail.com).

## Information collected and what it is used for

**Account information.** Reading lessons does not require an account. If you choose to register, the email address and display name you provide are stored; your password is kept only in hashed form, and the password itself is never stored. The account type (standard user) and the times the account was created and last updated are also recorded. This information is used to create your account, let you sign in, and give you access to the sections that require signing in (quizzes and practice).

When you register, you confirm that you accept the Terms of Use and have read this policy; the time the account was created is also the time of that confirmation.

**Quiz answers.** When you submit a quiz, your answers are sent to the server so they can be scored. The result is returned to your browser; your answers and your result are not saved to your account and are not stored permanently.

**Technical data.** As on most websites, each request you make to the site involves technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This data is processed by the site's server and by the network and hosting providers listed below, and is used to deliver the site to you, keep it secure and diagnose problems.

**Email correspondence.** If you send an email to the site, your email address and the content of your message are kept so that you can be answered.

The site does not sell your account information (your email address and display name). Data processed by advertising technologies is described separately under "Advertising" below.

## Cookies

The site's own application currently uses a session cookie (`JSESSIONID`) when a session is needed, for example when you open the sign-in or registration page or sign in; it keeps you signed in and protects forms against forged requests. Cloudflare, the site's network and security provider, may set its own essential security cookies when it needs to tell automated traffic apart or run a security check.

As of this update, the site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. If Google's advertising code is added to the site, advertising cookies may be used on pages that include it, even when no ad is visible on the page. Such cookies would be set by Google and its advertising partners, not by the site itself; see "Advertising" below.

## Third-party services

- **Cloudflare (network and security).** All requests to the site first pass through Cloudflare's network. In doing so Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser information; it protects the site against attacks, provides its domain name (DNS) service, and may receive connection error reports from your browser.
- **Railway (hosting).** The site's application and database run on Railway's infrastructure. Account information is stored there.
- **jsDelivr (content delivery network).** Styling and code-highlighting libraries are loaded from the jsDelivr CDN. Your browser connects to it directly, so it receives your IP address and browser information.
- **Google (email).** The site's contact address is a Gmail account; messages you send to it are stored in Google's email service.
- **Google (advertising).** If ads are shown on the site, they are served by Google AdSense. As of this update, Google's advertising code has not been added to the site. See "Advertising" below.

## Processing of data outside Türkiye

The site's application and database run on Railway's infrastructure in the United States. When you use the site, your technical data, and your account information if you have registered, are therefore processed and stored outside Türkiye, on servers in the United States.

Cloudflare and jsDelivr operate globally distributed networks; your request is usually handled at a location near you, which may be outside Türkiye. Messages you send to the Gmail address are stored on Google's global infrastructure. If Google's advertising code is added to the site, Google and its advertising partners will process the related data on their own global infrastructure.

## Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense to cover its running costs. As of this update, Google's advertising code has not been added to the site and no ads are shown. If it is added, this section applies to every page that includes it. Such a page can contact Google's servers and lead to cookies being set even when no ad is visible on it, for example when an ad space stays empty.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or to other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the Internet.
- Other third-party vendors and ad networks that work with Google may also use cookies to serve ads on the site.

**Your choices.** You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in [Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). According to Google, when ad personalization is off, Google does not use your information to personalize the ads it shows you; you still see ads, which may be based on things such as the topic of the page you are viewing or your general location. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; Google notes that this alone does not turn off personalized ads from Google unless you also change your ad settings.

**What is processed.** When a page that includes Google's advertising code loads, your browser connects to Google directly. Google receives technical data such as your IP address, the address of the page and information about your browser and device, and may set or read cookies.

Google's own documentation gives examples of the cookies its advertising products can use: `IDE` and `id` (to show Google ads on sites other than Google's, and to remember that personalized ads are turned off), `DSID` (to respect the ad setting of someone signed in to Google) and `_gads` (set on the domain of the site you are visiting). These are examples taken from Google's description, not a complete list, and not every one of them is necessarily set on this site. For the current list and how long each cookie lasts, see [the types of cookies Google uses](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies); for how Google uses the data, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Where consent is required.** In some places, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising cookies and personalized ads require your consent. Google's advertising code will not be added to the site before a consent message for visitors from those places is in place; this policy will then be updated to describe it, including how to change or withdraw your choice.

## Analytics

The site does not use an analytics service. If one is introduced, this policy will be updated first, and where the law requires it you will be asked for your consent.

## Retention

Account information is kept until you ask for your account to be deleted. Technical logs are kept for a limited period by Cloudflare and Railway, according to their own retention periods. Email correspondence is kept for as long as it is needed after your request has been resolved. If Google's advertising code is added, data collected by Google and its advertising partners will be kept by them according to their own retention periods.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in the European Union and the KVKK in Türkiye give you the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To use any of these rights, including deleting your account, write to [learnforgex@gmail.com](mailto:learnforgex@gmail.com).

If you find the response to your request unsatisfactory, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority where you live; in Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and personal information is not knowingly collected from them.
